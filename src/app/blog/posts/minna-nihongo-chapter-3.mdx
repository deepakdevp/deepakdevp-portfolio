## Japanese Verb Basics

Before diving into conjugation, here are the fundamentals:

### 1. Dictionary Form vs Polite Form

Japanese verbs have multiple forms. For beginners, you need to know two:

- **Dictionary form (plain):** 食べる (taberu) - to eat
- **Polite form (ます-form):** 食べます (tabemasu) - (I) eat

Chapter 3 focuses on **ます-form** because it's polite and appropriate for most situations.

### 2. No Subject Conjugation

English changes verbs based on subject:
- I eat / he eat**s** / they eat

Japanese doesn't:
- 私は食べます (I eat)
- 彼は食べます (He eats)
- 彼らは食べます (They eat)

**Same verb form for all subjects!** This was such a relief.

### 3. Three Verb Groups

Here's where it gets tricky. Japanese verbs fall into three groups, and you conjugate them differently:

**Group 1 (う-verbs):** End in -u sounds (く、ぐ、す、つ、ぬ、ぶ、む、る)
- 書く (kaku) → 書きます (kakimasu) - to write
- 飲む (nomu) → 飲みます (nomimasu) - to drink
- 話す (hanasu) → 話します (hanashimasu) - to speak

**Group 2 (る-verbs):** End in -eru or -iru
- 食べる (taberu) → 食べます (tabemasu) - to eat
- 見る (miru) → 見ます (mimasu) - to see

**Group 3 (Irregular):** Only two verbs!
- する (suru) → します (shimasu) - to do
- 来る (kuru) → 来ます (kimasu) - to come

The challenge: some う-verbs **look like** る-verbs but aren't. You have to memorize them.

Example:
- 帰る (kaeru) - looks like Group 2, but it's Group 1
- 帰ります (kaerimasu) - to return home

## ます-Form Conjugation

To make polite present/future tense, convert dictionary form to ます-form:

### Group 1 (う-verbs):
Replace final -u with -imasu

- 書く (kaku) → 書き**ます** (kakimasu)
- 飲む (nomu) → 飲み**ます** (nomimasu)
- 行く (iku) → 行き**ます** (ikimasu)

### Group 2 (る-verbs):
Drop る, add ます

- 食べる (taberu) → 食べ**ます** (tabemasu)
- 見る (miru) → 見**ます** (mimasu)

### Group 3 (Irregular):
Just memorize them

- する → します
- 来る → 来ます

## Negative Form: ません

To make negative, replace ます with **ません**:

- 食べます (tabemasu) - eat → 食べません (tabemasen) - don't eat
- 飲みます (nomimasu) - drink → 飲みません (nomimasen) - don't drink
- 行きます (ikimasu) - go → 行きません (ikimasen) - don't go

Examples:
```
私は肉を食べません。
(Watashi wa niku o tabemasen)
I don't eat meat.

今日は会社へ行きません。
(Kyō wa kaisha e ikimasen)
I'm not going to the company today.
```

## Particles with Verbs

Chapter 3 introduces verb-specific particles:

### を (o) - Direct Object Marker

Marks what receives the action of the verb.

```
コーヒーを飲みます。
(Kōhī o nomimasu)
(I) drink coffee.
```

Pattern: [object] **を** [verb]

More examples:
- 本を読みます (hon o yomimasu) - read a book
- 音楽を聞きます (ongaku o kikimasu) - listen to music
- テレビを見ます (terebi o mimasu) - watch TV

### で (de) - Location of Action

Marks where the action takes place.

```
会社で働きます。
(Kaisha de hatarakimasu)
(I) work at the company.
```

More examples:
- 家で勉強します (uchi de benkyō shimasu) - study at home
- レストランで食べます (resutoran de tabemasu) - eat at a restaurant
- 図書館で本を読みます (toshokan de hon o yomimasu) - read books at the library

### へ (e) - Direction

Marks destination (pronounced "e" not "he").

```
会社へ行きます。
(Kaisha e ikimasu)
(I) go to the company.
```

More examples:
- 家へ帰ります (uchi e kaerimasu) - return home
- 東京へ来ます (Tōkyō e kimasu) - come to Tokyo

**Note:** に (ni) can also mark destination and is more common in modern Japanese, but Chapter 3 teaches へ first.

```
朝6時に起きます。
(Asa roku-ji ni okimasu)

コーヒーを飲みます。
(Kōhī o nomimasu)

7時に家を出ます。
(Shichi-ji ni uchi o demasu)

(Kaisha e ikimasu)
I go to the company.

会社でコードを書きます。
(Kaisha de kōdo o kakimasu)

(Hiru ni resutoran de hiru-gohan o tabemasu)

(Gogo, mītingu o shimasu)
In the afternoon, I have meetings.

(Shichi-ji ni kaisha o demasu)

家へ帰ります。

(Jūichi-ji ni nemasu)
```

## Time Expressions

Chapter 3 introduces time words needed for daily routines:

**Times of day:**
- 朝 (asa) - morning
- 昼 (hiru) - noon/daytime
- 午後 (gogo) - afternoon
- 夜 (yoru) - evening/night
- 毎朝 (mai-asa) - every morning
- 毎日 (mainichi) - every day

**Frequency:**
- 時々 (tokidoki) - sometimes
- よく (yoku) - often
- いつも (itsumo) - always
- 全然〜ません (zenzen ~masen) - not at all

Examples:
```
毎朝コーヒーを飲みます。
(Mai-asa kōhī o nomimasu)
I drink coffee every morning.

時々レストランで食べます。
(Tokidoki resutoran de tabemasu)
I sometimes eat at restaurants.

全然運動しません。
(Zenzen undō shimasen)
I don't exercise at all. (sadly true...)
```

## Common Mistakes I Made

### Mistake 1: Wrong Verb Group

❌ 帰る is Group 2, so 帰ます
✅ 帰る is Group 1, so 帰**り**ます

**Fix:** Memorize which verbs are Group 1 vs Group 2. There's no perfect rule.

### Mistake 2: Particle Confusion

❌ 会社を働きます (wrong particle)
✅ 会社**で**働きます

**Fix:** を for direct objects, で for location of action.

### Mistake 3: Forgetting を

❌ コーヒー飲みます (missing を)
✅ コーヒー**を**飲みます

**Fix:** Always use を before transitive verbs.

My wife caught this one: "In casual speech, you can drop を, but for JLPT N5, always include it."

## Real-World Practice

I tried using verbs at a Japanese grocery store:

**Me:** これを買います。(I'll buy this)

**Cashier:** かしこまりました。(Understood)

Simple, but effective. The cashier understood me!

Another day, I tried at a coffee shop:

**Me:** コーヒーを飲みます。(I'll have coffee)

**Barista:** サイズは？(Size?)

**Me:** (panic - didn't learn sizes yet)

**Barista:** スモール？ミディアム？ラージ？

**Me:** ミディアム！

**Barista:** (smiles) かしこまりました。

Small victories. Each one builds confidence.
