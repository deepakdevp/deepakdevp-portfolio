## Overview

**Travorier** (formerly CarryShare) is a crowdsourced logistics SaaS platform that bridges two everyday realities: travelers flying with unused luggage capacity and people needing to ship packages affordably.

Traditional international courier services charge ₹3,000–5,000 for a 2kg parcel from Mumbai to Dubai — and they take 3–5 days. Meanwhile, millions of travelers fly the same route weekly carrying far less than their allowed weight. Travorier turns that wasted capacity into a win-win: senders get same-day or next-day delivery at a fraction of the cost, and travelers earn ₹500–1,500 per trip.

The platform acts purely as a **discovery and verification engine**. It is not a courier — it enables verified peer-to-peer agreements between users.

## Problem Statement

| Pain Point | Traditional Couriers | Travorier |
|---|---|---|
| Cost (2kg Mumbai → Dubai) | ₹3,000–5,000 | ₹99 unlock + ₹500 (P2P) |
| Delivery Time | 3–5 days | Same-day / next-day |
| Trust | Corporate brand | Verified ID + PNR |
| Environmental Impact | Dedicated freight | Utilizes existing trips |

---

## How It Works

1. **Traveler posts a trip** — route, date, available kg, price per kg, and optionally verifies their PNR (boarding pass)
2. **Sender searches and finds a match** — filters by date, destination, price; unlocks traveler contact for ₹99
3. **Both parties connect via in-app chat** — coordinate package handover location & time
4. **Physical inspection at handover** — traveler records a video or photos of the unsealed package (proof of contents)
5. **QR code confirmation at delivery** — recipient scans traveler's QR code to confirm delivery

## Key Features

### 🔐 Trust & Safety System
- Manual ID verification (government ID + selfie) with admin approval
- PNR verification via boarding pass screenshot upload
- Physical inspection protocol: traveler must record unsealed package contents
- QR-based delivery confirmation — no QR = no "delivered" status
- 24-hour chat auto-lock after flight departure (prevents harassment/spam)

### 💳 Credit-Based Access Model
- ₹99 to unlock a traveler's contact details (prevents spam browse-without-intent)
- ₹49 trip listing fee (ensures only genuine travelers post)
- Bulk credit packs: 5 credits (10% off) or 10 credits (20% off), 1-year validity
- Stripe payment integration with test-mode staging environment

### 🏆 Gamified Trust Profiles
- Trust score calculated from completed deliveries and reviews
- "Frequent Flyer" badge after 5+ deliveries
- Weighted reviews: PNR-verified trips count 2× in trust score calculation
- Traveler and sender ratings visible on all profile cards

## Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Unlock Fee | ₹99 / unlock | Sender pays per traveler contact |
| Trip Listing Fee | ₹49 / trip | Traveler pays per trip posted |
| Trip Boost (V2) | ₹199 / 48hrs | Top placement in search results |
| 5-Credit Pack | 10% discount | Bulk unlock credits |
| 10-Credit Pack | 20% discount | Bulk unlock credits |

**Platform takes 0% commission on delivery fees** — the traveler-sender agreement is peer-to-peer.
